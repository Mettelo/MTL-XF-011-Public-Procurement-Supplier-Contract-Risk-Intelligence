## Commission

The directorate publishes and manages a large volume of procurement activity, but commercial leaders do not have one governed view of supplier concentration, repeated awards, fragmented buying, contract timing and SME participation. Existing reports are notice-centric rather than decision-centric, so teams cannot consistently distinguish genuine commercial exposure from simple high-volume activity.

**Decision to support:** Which suppliers, categories and contract groups should commercial leadership investigate first for concentration exposure, upcoming re-procurement pressure, fragmented demand or weak SME participation?

This is not a dashboard-only exercise. The team is expected to deliver discovery, requirements, governance, engineering, analytical assessment, decision support, assurance/UAT and handover.
