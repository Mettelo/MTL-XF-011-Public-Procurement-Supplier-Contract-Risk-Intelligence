## The Commission

The organisation manages a large portfolio of procurement notices and supplier relationships, but senior leaders cannot reliably identify supplier concentration, fragmented purchasing, contract expiry exposure, SME participation or opportunities for commercial intervention from one governed view.

**Executive decision:** Where should commercial leadership intervene first to reduce supplier dependency, prepare for contract expiry, consolidate fragmented demand and improve procurement outcomes?

This is not a dashboard-only exercise. Teams are expected to demonstrate an end-to-end professional delivery lifecycle across business analysis, data governance, engineering, analysis, BI/decision support and assurance.
